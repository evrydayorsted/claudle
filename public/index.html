How to play

Every day, Claudle shows you something an AI said. Your job is to guess the exact 5-word instruction that made it say that, in 5 tries.

Each guess must be 5 words. After you submit one, every word gets colored:

EXPLAIN
OCEANS
USING
EASY
TERMS
  • Green — right word, right spot
  • Yellow — a synonym, or the right word in the wrong spot
  • Red — way off

Example: the real prompt is "Explain volcanoes using simple words." You guessed "Explain oceans using easy terms." EXPLAIN and USING are exactly right, EASY turns yellow because it's a synonym of "simple," and OCEANS and TERMS are way off.

Stuck? The "Reveal a word" button shows up to 3 words of the answer for you.
